How to read this: each bar is one approval track. The left end is roughly how far ahead of launch you need to start it; the right end is when it should be finished. Bars that run past the launch line continue after launch (operations, reporting, reentry, product sales). A red outline marks the critical path, the track most likely to delay launch if it slips.

Durations are rough estimates for class discussion, based on published review times and typical industry experience. Real timelines vary widely by mission and agency workload.
